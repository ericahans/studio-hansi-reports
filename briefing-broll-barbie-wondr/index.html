Shooting Brief · B-Roll Scenes

Barbie The Dream Experience
& WONDR Experience

Family-focused b-roll direction — grandma, mom & kids interacting across both venues. Kid is always the protagonist.

General direction
Movement and interaction that feels real, without messing up or damaging the set.
Grandma and mom mimicking what the kids would do (e.g. diving into the ball pit).
Cross-generational interaction: adult helps kid (helmet) and kid helps adult (hat on grandma).
Whole family in frame, interacting with the environment and exchanging happy looks — the classic "grandma in love with her grandchild" look.
Barbie → pretend to be "playing" (driving, camping).
WONDR → prioritize the rooms with the strongest visual hook: Kaleidoscope and WONDRscope.
Mandatory
Kid is always the protagonist of the scene · Respect the 9:16 safe area in every framing
